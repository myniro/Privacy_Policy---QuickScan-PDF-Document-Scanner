Welcome to QuickScan – PDF & Document Scanner ("we", "our", or "the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy strictly explains what permissions our app uses, why they are needed, and how your data is handled.

🔒 Zero Server Upload Policy: All document scanning, image edge cropping, filters, signatures, stamps, and PDF exports are processed 100% locally on your device. We do NOT operate external document servers, and we never collect, upload, or share your scanned documents or personal files.

1. Scope of the Application

QuickScan is an Android productivity application designed to help users scan paper documents, receipts, business cards, ID cards, and notes into high-quality digital PDF files. Key features included in the application are:

  • Capturing documents using your device's camera or selecting existing photos via the Android Photo Picker.
  • Automatic and manual document corner detection and perspective cropping.
  • Applying document enhancement filters (e.g., Magic Color, Black & White, Grayscale, Sharp).
  • Adding custom drawn digital signatures and predefined status stamps directly to pages.
  • Protecting scanned documents on-device with an optional 4-digit Security PIN.
  • Exporting multi-page scans into standardized PDF files or individual JPEG images.

2. Android Permissions We Use & Why

Our app requests only the minimum permissions necessary to perform its core functions:

Permission Type Exact Purpose
android.permission.CAMERA Runtime Permission Used strictly to allow you to photograph physical documents within the scan screen. The camera stream is analyzed on-device only; photos are never sent to external servers.
android.permission.INTERNET Install-Time Permission Required to connect to Google Play Billing servers for VIP purchases/subscriptions, and to serve advertisements via Google AdMob for free users.
com.android.vending.BILLING Install-Time Permission Used to initiate official Google Play in-app purchases (Lifetime VIP plan) and recurring subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly VIP plans).

3. Local Storage & Zero External Transmission

All document data remains on your physical Android device:

  • Local File Storage: Scanned pages, cropped images, and exported PDF files are saved directly inside your device's private app directory or public Downloads directory (when you choose to export).
  • No Cloud Account Required: You do not need to register, create a username, or provide an email address to use the scanning and PDF creation features.
  • Device Security PIN: If you enable the Document Security PIN, the PIN hash is stored solely in local Android Encrypted/Private SharedPreferences. It never leaves your device.
  • Sharing: Your documents are only transmitted to third parties if you manually use the native Android "Share" button to send a PDF via email, messaging, or cloud drives of your choice.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The application incorporates only two trusted, official third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC): Handles the payment processing for QuickScan VIP upgrades. All credit card numbers, payment methods, and billing addresses are handled exclusively by Google Play. We never see, collect, or store your financial details. Learn more: Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): For users on the Free tier, non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements are displayed. Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) and general diagnostic data to deliver advertisements according to Google's Privacy Policy. Learn more: Google Advertising Privacy Policy.
    *Note: QuickScan VIP users have all advertisements completely disabled.*

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all document data is stored locally:

  • Deleting a page or document inside QuickScan permanently removes it from your device storage immediately.
  • Uninstalling QuickScan or choosing "Clear Data" in Android System Settings permanently deletes all local scans and settings stored by the application.

6. Children's Privacy

QuickScan is a general audience document utility and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
