Welcome to QuickScan – PDF & Document Scanner ("we", "our", or "the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. 1. Scope of the Application

QuickScan is an Android productivity application designed to help users scan paper documents, receipts, business cards, ID cards, and notes into high-quality digital PDF files. Key features included in the application are:

  • Capturing documents using your device's camera or selecting existing photos via the Android Photo Picker.
  • Automatic and manual document corner detection and perspective cropping.
  • Applying document enhancement filters (e.g., Magic Color, Black & White, Grayscale, Sharp).
  • Adding custom drawn digital signatures and predefined status stamps directly to pages.
  • Protecting scanned documents on-device with an optional 4-digit Security PIN.
  • Exporting multi-page scans into standardized PDF files or individual JPEG images.
  • Optional user account creation and sign-in to manage your profile and synchronize VIP subscription entitlements.

2. User Account Registration & Third-Party Authentication

QuickScan provides an optional user account feature. You can use the document scanning and PDF creation features as a Guest without creating an account. However, if you choose to sign up or log in, we utilize secure authentication mechanisms as described below:

  • Third-Party Authentication (Google Sign-In / Google Identity Services & Firebase Authentication):
    When you choose "Sign in with Google" or "Sign up with Google", authentication is handled securely by Google Identity Services and Firebase Authentication (Google LLC).
    Information Received from Third-Party Login:
    • Google User ID (UID): A unique pseudonymous identifier used to recognize your account session.
    • Email Address: Used strictly to identify your account and verify your VIP status.
    • Display Name & Profile Picture URL: Used solely to personalize your profile header within the app.
    Note: We never access, request, or receive your Google account password or contacts.
  • Email & Password Login:
    If you register using an email address and password, your password is never stored in plain text. Passwords are secured locally using unique cryptographic per-user salts and SHA-256 hashing, and can also be authenticated through Firebase Authentication.
  • Purpose of Login Details:
    Your login credentials and profile details are used strictly for:
    • Authenticating your identity and maintaining your active sign-in session.
    • Synchronizing and restoring your QuickScan VIP entitlement (e.g., across device re-installs).
    • Allowing you to update your profile details or securely delete your account at any time.

4. Local Storage & Zero External Transmission

All document data remains on your physical Android device:

  • Local File Storage: Scanned pages, cropped images, and exported PDF files are saved directly inside your device's private app directory or public Downloads directory (when you choose to export).
  • No Cloud Document Upload: Your physical documents and scanned images are never sent to our servers, Google, or any third party.
  • Device Security PIN: If you enable the Document Security PIN, the PIN hash is stored solely in local Android Encrypted/Private SharedPreferences. It never leaves your device.
  • Manual Sharing: Your documents are only shared with third parties if you manually use the native Android "Share" button to send a PDF via email, messaging, or cloud drives of your own choice.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

We respect your right to control your personal data:

  • In-App Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your user account directly inside the app at any time by navigating to Settings > Account Security & Deletion > Delete Account. This permanently wipes your user profile, authentication credentials, and session tokens.
  • Document Deletion: Deleting a document inside QuickScan permanently removes the file from device storage immediately.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling QuickScan or selecting "Clear Data" in Android System Settings completely erases all locally saved documents, database entries, and settings.

7. Children's Privacy
